Atlas Interior

Internal API client of the YouTube channel Atlas Interior

Atlas Interior is an educational YouTube channel in Brazilian Portuguese about self-knowledge through psychology, philosophy and spirituality: youtube.com/channel/UCwobZmSm57JO-m07syIu1oA.

The Atlas Interior API client (Google Cloud project atlasinterior) is an internal tool used only by the channel owner, signed in with their own Google Account, to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, manage their metadata, thumbnails, banner and watermark, reply to comments on the channel's own videos and read the channel's own analytics. It is not offered to the public.
